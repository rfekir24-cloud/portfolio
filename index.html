Expected FOMC Tone and Market Reactions

End-to-end empirical research pipeline testing whether 30-minute intraday movements in the S&P 500, Treasury yields, and OIS rates respond to the unexpected hawkish/dovish tone of Federal Reserve statements.

CORE INNOVATION IDENTITY
tone_innovation[t] = actual_tone[t] − predicted_tone[t]

Key Architecture & Findings

  • Continuous NLP Scoring: Uses Delta-Consistent Scoring (DCS, 0=dovish to 1=hawkish) benchmarked against FedNLP manual labels and transparent YAML dictionaries.
  • Walk-Forward Forecasting: Generates strictly out-of-sample tone forecasts from pre-announcement FRED macro series, VIX, Treasury yields, dollar index, and SPY features using Persistence, Ridge, ElasticNet, and Shallow Random Forests.
  • Intraday Event-Study Regressions: 30-minute SF Fed USMPD window regressions with HC3 heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors and futures-based policy surprise controls (MP1, MP2).
  • Production-Grade Packaging: Installable fomc-tone CLI, 5 reproducible Jupyter notebooks, pinned external commits, and 15+ unit & research-design tests.

Customer Analytics & Explainable ML Scikit-Learn • SHAP

Online Retail Customer Engagement Baseline

Leak-safe predictive modeling framework estimating 30-day customer repurchase probability on the UCI Online Retail transactional dataset, with calibrated probability outputs and feature explainability.

TEMPORAL SPLIT PROTOCOL
History ≤ P80(InvoiceDate) → Target ∈ (Cutoff, Cutoff + 30d]

Key Architecture & Findings

  • Strict Leak Prevention: Enforces an 80th-percentile temporal cutoff on invoice timestamps so customer features only use historical transactions prior to the prediction window.
  • Behavioral Feature Store: Engineers Recency, Frequency, Monetary (RFM) metrics, basket statistics, unique product counts, interpurchase intervals, and cancellation ratios.
  • Calibrated Classification: Benchmarks Logistic Regression and Gradient Boosting pipelines across ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, optimal F1 precision-recall threshold, Brier score, and reliability curves.
  • Model Explainability: Combines linear coefficient inspection, tree feature importances, and SHAP attributions with serialized model artifacts (model.joblib).

RESEARCH STANDARDS

Engineering & Econometric Principles

01

Zero Look-Ahead Leakage

Every forecasting and classification pipeline enforces strict chronological boundaries—expanding walk-forward windows for macro events and percentile cutoffs for transactional cohorts.

02

Econometric & Calibration Rigor

Models are audited beyond point accuracy using HC3 robust inference, monetary policy futures surprise controls (MP1/MP2), precision-recall thresholds, and Brier calibration curves.

03

Deterministic Reproducibility

External benchmarks are pinned to immutable Git commits, data provenance is checksum-verified, and research-design invariants are enforced via automated test suites.
